Pinpoint Weather A Telegraph miner · testnet

Weather answers that say which place they are for.

Ask for storm risk, a forecast, the temperature now, or what the weather was on a past day. Every answer names the exact place it is for, with region, country and coordinates. If it can't tell which place you mean, it refuses instead of guessing.

    The full answer
    
    
    
  
    

  
    What you can ask
    
  
    
    
    Storm risk
    A grade from LOW to SEVERE, the peak gust, the total rain and the exact hours covered, for the next 1 to 240 hours.
    GET /storm-alert?location=…&window_hours=24
    
    
    Forecast
    High, low, the peak chance of rain and the conditions in plain words, for the next 1 to 240 hours.
    GET /forecast?location=…&window_hours=24
    
    
    Temperature now
    The current air temperature in °C, when it was read, and a second source to compare.
    GET /temperature?location=…
    
    
    A past day
    The recorded high, low and rain for one finished day at that place. Use it to check a forecast.
    GET /verify?location=…&date=2026-11-15
    
  
    

  
    When it refuses
    
  
    
    
      
      
    • The region has no such town. "Paris, Kansas" is refused. It is never swapped for Paris, France or Paris, Texas.
      • 
      
    • Several towns of similar size share the name. "Springfield" is refused with the list, so you can pick one.
      • 
      
    • The name is a country or a whole region. A forecast needs a point.
      • 
      
    • The day hasn't ended there yet, by that place's own clock.
      • 
      
    • A weather source didn't answer. Nothing is filled in with zeros or guesses.
      • 
    
    
    
    A refusal is an error reply (4xx or 502), so Telegraph does not bill the buyer for it.
    
  
    

  
    How the storm grade is worked out
    
  
    
    
    
      
    What is measuredLOWMODERATEHIGHSEVERE
    
      
    Peak wind gust, km/hunder 4040 to 6262 to 8989 or more
    
      
    Wettest 24 hours, mmunder 1010 to 3030 to 8080 or more
    
      
    Wettest single hour, mmunder 7.67.6 to 2020 to 5050 or more
    
      
    Snowfall in 24 hours, cmunder 55 to 1515 to 4040 or more
    
      
    Thunderstorm forecastyeswith hail
    
      
    Official US storm alertMinorModerateSevereExtreme
    
    
    
    
    The grade is the worst of these. Every factor is returned in the answer under drivers, so you can recompute it. It is a rule, not a probability and not an official warning.
    
  
    

  
    Where the numbers come from
    
  
    
    
      
      
    • Forecasts: Open-Meteo, with three models side by side (its best match, NOAA GFS and DWD ICON) and how far apart they are.
      • 
      
    • Official alerts: the US National Weather Service, for places in the United States only. Storm alerts (wind, flood, thunderstorm, winter storm, hurricane) raise the grade. Others, such as a freeze warning, are listed but do not.
      • 
      
    • Past days: the Open-Meteo archive, with NASA POWER as a second record and the difference between them.
      • 
      
    • Place names: GeoNames through Open-Meteo, then OpenStreetMap if the town is missing.
      • 
    
    
    
    No language model writes any part of an answer. Every answer lists its sources as links you can open.
