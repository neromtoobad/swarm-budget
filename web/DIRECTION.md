# Direction: field ledger

Reference: zipline.com (drone delivery). Taken as principles only: warm paper background
(#F7F4E8-ish), black ink, a bold condensed display face over a plain grotesk, the landscape carries the mood.

Ours: a low-poly 3D farm on the left, a running ledger on the right. Every paid answer is a coin that rises
from the machine that bought it; a rejected answer is stamped red and drops. Tokens:

- field greens #6F8F3E / #8DA858, soil #C8AE7E, road #D9CFB8
- accepted #2F7D4F, rejected #C4422B, held #B7791F, coin #E3A82B
